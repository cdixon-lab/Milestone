Featured Project: Housing Affordability

Housing Affordability Research

This project explores housing affordability and the challenges that rising housing costs create for individuals and families.

I researched possible solutions including affordable housing development, rental assistance programs, zoning changes, and additional investment in housing resources.

  • Affordable housing can help create more stable communities.
  • High housing costs can make it difficult for families to find safe housing.
  • Government funding and rental assistance can help address housing needs.
  • Zoning changes and nonprofit programs can support affordable housing development.
