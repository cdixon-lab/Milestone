Affordable Housing

Affordable housing helps make homeownership and stable housing more accessible to first-time buyers, families, and low-income households.

The United States has a shortage of affordable rental homes, which can make it difficult for low-income households to find safe and stable housing.

Government funding, zoning changes, rental assistance, and nonprofit housing programs can help increase the availability of affordable housing.

Learning about housing affordability can help communities understand the challenges people face and support solutions that make housing more accessible.
